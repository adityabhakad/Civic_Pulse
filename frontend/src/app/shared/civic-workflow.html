THE CIVICPULSE SYSTEM

From report to resolution

Every report moves through a clear, trackable process.

  1. STEP 01

    Report an issue

    A citizen describes a local problem and its location.

  2. STEP 02

    Report is recorded

    CivicPulse saves it so it can be reviewed and tracked.

  3. STEP 03

    Admin reviews

    The local team reviews the report and updates its status.

  4. STEP 04

    Progress is visible

    The citizen follows progress until the issue is resolved.
